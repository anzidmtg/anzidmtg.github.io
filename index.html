anzidbot

A chat bot for the anzidmtg trading card game tournament broadcasts.

anzidbot runs alongside the live production software for anzidmtg's Riftbound and Magic: The Gathering tournament streams. It reads the stream's public live chat, and lets viewers put things on screen and ask what is being played.

What it does

  • !card <name> or [[name]] — shows that trading card on the broadcast for a few seconds.
  • !decklists — replies with the match currently on stream and where to find the players' decklists.
  • A small number of operator-only commands, used by the channel owner to load decklists onto the scoreboard.

Where it runs

On the broadcast machine operated by the channel owner, for that channel's own streams. It is not a service other people sign up for, and it has no accounts, logins or user-facing website beyond this page.

Why it needs access to YouTube

To read the live chat of the channel's own broadcasts, and to post replies in that chat as the bot account. It does not read, upload, edit or delete videos, and it does not touch any channel other than the one it is streaming for.
